Policy notes and evidence are presented here as readable summaries. They will evolve as research, consultation and technical review continue.

Draft policy notes

Heritage

Working material for evidence gathering and discussion, not adopted policy.

Hinton Martell

Draft note

Development should respond to the settlement's historic form, listed buildings and their settings. Evidence work will identify important approaches, views, boundary treatments and landscape relationships that contribute to local character.

Witchampton

Draft note

Policy work will consider the conservation area, historic buildings, street pattern and the relationship between the village and surrounding landscape. Design guidance should reinforce locally distinctive scale, materials and enclosure.

Parish-wide assets

Evidence scope

The evidence base will map designated and locally valued heritage assets across the plan area, including listed buildings, conservation zones, archaeological features, the Cursus, the Roman Road and historic landscape patterns.

Evidence summary

Housing need

Housing Register & Local Need Data Summary

The Housing Working Group is reviewing Dorset Council housing register information alongside locally gathered evidence. The analysis will look at household size, affordability, tenure, accessibility requirements and evidence of a connection to the plan area.
