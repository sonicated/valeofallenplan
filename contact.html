Enquiries & feedback

Bring your local knowledge
into the conversation.

Tell the Steering Group about local needs, valued places and practical ideas that should inform the neighbourhood plan.

Before you write

Your views matter

Residents, landowners, local organisations and businesses can submit thoughts at any stage. Useful feedback explains the place or issue concerned, why it matters and any evidence or practical solution you would like the group to consider.

Prefer to speak locally?

You can also share views through your Parish Council representative, who can raise relevant neighbourhood planning matters with the Steering Group.

Planning application comments must still be submitted through Dorset Council's formal planning process. This form is for neighbourhood plan matters.
