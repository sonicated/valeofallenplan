Community survey results

What residents
want to protect and improve.

These themes were gathered through a physical leaflet consultation delivered to households across all nine Vale of Allen settlements.

With thanks

Made possible by our community

The Steering Group is deeply grateful to every resident who took the time to respond. Each contribution has helped build a clearer picture of the priorities, concerns and hopes shared across the Vale of Allen.

We also extend our sincere thanks to all the volunteers who made the consultation possible by distributing leaflets across the nine settlements, encouraging participation and helping bring the responses together. Their time, care and local knowledge have given this work a stronger, more representative foundation.

What the responses show

Place, housing and local needs

Quality of life

When residents scored aspects of life in the Vale, the highest-ranked factors combined social wellbeing with the natural setting.

  1. 1A friendly and safe environment
  2. 2Open and green spaces in the village
  3. 3Proximity to rivers and woodland
  4. 4A sense of community

80.0% of respondents described the current age profile of their village as “about right”.

Housing preferences

239 returns selected one of the two lowest rates of housebuilding: less than one, or between one and two homes per year. This is 89% of all returned questionnaires and 91% of those answering this question.

Less than 1 per year146
Between 1 and 2 per year93
Between 2 and 4 per year15
More than 4 per year8

In the weighted ranking of potential housing sources, brownfield sites ranked first, followed by individual plots or a development. Greenfield sites received the lowest weighted score.

Reading the results

Percentages are rounded to one decimal place. Question totals vary where a household did not answer. Weighted-score questions show relative ranking rather than a share of respondents, and multiple-choice questions may total more than 100%.
