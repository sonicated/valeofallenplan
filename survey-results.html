Community survey results

What residents
want to protect and improve.

These themes were gathered through a physical leaflet consultation delivered to households across all nine Vale of Allen settlements.

Key findings

A clear direction

Responses show strong support for proportionate development and careful stewardship of the area's rural character.

Theme 01

Housing priorities

Residents favour small-scale housing that responds to evidenced local need and supports people with a connection to the area. There is concern about large speculative developments that could overwhelm village form, local services or landscape setting.

Theme 02

Environment & heritage

Protecting village character, conservation boundaries and historic assets is a high public priority. Responses also value dark skies, biodiversity, open views and the landscape features that distinguish the Vale of Allen.

Theme 03

Business & infrastructure

There is support for the local rural economy and agriculture, alongside better infrastructure for home working. Residents also want practical improvements to local travel connections and safe, useful walking routes.

Small scale

Development shaped around demonstrable local need.

Character first

Village identity, heritage and landscape valued together.

Rural vitality

Support for farming, local enterprise and connectivity.

From feedback to policy

Working groups are testing these themes against evidence and planning requirements.
